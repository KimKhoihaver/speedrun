SPEEDRUNNING

Learn the Basics of Speedrunning

Minecraft speedrunning is about completing the game as efficiently as possible. A good run is not simply about moving quickly. You also need to make decisions about resources, structures, travel, combat, inventory management, and risk. The biggest improvement usually comes from understanding the route rather than simply trying to move faster.

01

Fast Early Game

Gather only the resources you actually need. Wood, stone, food, iron, and basic tools should be collected quickly without spending too much time on unnecessary materials.

02

Nether Preparation

Before entering the Nether, think about your equipment and resources. A reliable food supply, useful blocks, weapons, and enough materials for your route can prevent unnecessary deaths.

03

Stronghold & End

Once you have the necessary Nether resources, the next goal is locating the stronghold and preparing for the End. Efficient navigation becomes especially important here.

TUTORIALS

Minecraft Tutorials

These tutorials focus on useful Minecraft techniques that can help with both ordinary survival worlds and speedrunning.

TUTORIAL 01

Getting Started

When you create a new world, your first priority should be obtaining wood and basic tools. Turn logs into planks, craft a crafting table, and make wooden tools only long enough to obtain stone. Once you have stone, upgrade your tools and start gathering food.

TIP: Do not spend too long collecting materials you do not immediately need. Early-game efficiency is mostly about keeping your goals simple.
TUTORIAL 02

Finding Iron Efficiently

Iron is extremely useful because it can be turned into buckets, shields, tools, armor, and many other items. In a normal survival world, explore caves and exposed stone areas while keeping track of your surroundings. For speedrunning, iron is particularly valuable because a bucket can dramatically change how quickly you can create and travel through a Nether portal setup.

TIP: Prioritize the iron needed for your immediate route instead of trying to obtain a full set of iron equipment.
TUTORIAL 03

Nether Preparation

The Nether is one of the most important dimensions for progressing toward the End. You may need blaze rods from a fortress and gold-related resources depending on the strategy you are using. Before entering, make sure your inventory is organized and that you have enough blocks and food to handle unexpected situations.

TIP: Always know where your portal is. Getting lost in the Nether can cost far more time than the resources you gained.
TUTORIAL 04

Stronghold Preparation

After obtaining the resources needed for your route, locate the stronghold. Keep your inventory organized and prepare enough food and blocks before entering. Once inside, explore carefully while looking for the End portal room.

TIP: Remember important coordinates or landmarks so you can return to useful locations if necessary.

COMMANDS

Useful Minecraft Commands

Commands are especially useful when testing farms, practicing mechanics, creating maps, or experimenting with different Minecraft systems.

/gamemode creative

Switches the player to Creative mode when commands are enabled.

/gamemode survival

Switches the player to Survival mode.

/time set day

Changes the world time to daytime.

/weather clear

Clears the current weather.

/locate structure minecraft:stronghold

In versions where this syntax is supported, searches for a stronghold.

FARMS

Useful Survival Farms

Farms allow you to turn renewable resources into reliable supplies. They are especially useful in long-term survival worlds.

🌾 Wheat Farm

A simple wheat farm provides food and wheat for breeding animals. It is easy to build early in a survival world and can later be expanded.

🐄 Animal Farm

Keeping cows or other livestock gives you a renewable source of food and useful materials.

🧟 Mob Farm

Mob farms can provide drops and experience while reducing the amount of time spent manually fighting mobs.

⚙️ Iron Farm

Iron farms can provide a renewable iron supply for tools, buckets, rails, hoppers, and many other useful items.

SURVIVAL

Survival Knowledge

Survive Longer. Prepare Better.

Survival Minecraft rewards preparation. Even if your goal is not speedrunning, having organized resources and a clear plan makes dangerous situations much easier to handle.

Some useful habits include:

  • Keep important items organized.
  • Carry enough food before exploring.
  • Bring blocks when entering dangerous areas.
  • Keep track of important coordinates.
  • Avoid carrying everything valuable when exploring somewhere dangerous.
  • Prepare equipment before entering the Nether or End.
